<section id="bio">


<h2>Biography</h2>


<p>


Jett Tarwater is a contemporary multidisciplinary artist whose work explores

identity, perception, and the relationship between technology and traditional

artmaking. Through painting, photography, mixed media, and digital processes,

their practice examines how everyday experiences become transformed into visual

narratives.


Drawing inspiration from architecture, nature, and human interaction, Jett

creates immersive works that encourage viewers to reconsider familiar spaces

through new perspectives.


</p>


</section>




<section id="statement">


<h2>Artist Statement</h2>


<p>


My artistic practice investigates the intersection of memory, environment,

and material. Every piece begins with observation and develops through

experimentation, allowing texture, color, and composition to evolve naturally.


Rather than seeking definitive answers, my work encourages reflection.

By combining traditional techniques with contemporary digital processes,

I aim to create works that invite curiosity while revealing the subtle

relationships between objects, spaces, and personal experience.


</p>


</section>
